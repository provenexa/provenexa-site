What we collect

From you:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send what you asked for.
  • Your name, company and role, if you choose to give them.

  • Your plan and payment status. We do not get your card, bank or UPI details: our payment partner handles those.

When you use the service:

  • Which link or advert brought you to us, read from the link itself.
  • Page visit counts from our analytics service, GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and collects no personal data.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and show you the moves you asked for.
  • To send your alerts and the weekly mail. You can stop the weekly mail any time with the link in each mail.
  • To bill you and issue invoices.
  • To understand which of our adverts and posts work.
  • To keep the service safe and to meet our legal duties.

We use your data with your consent, and where the law allows it for these purposes. You can withdraw consent at any time. Some features may then stop working, such as alerts.

We never sell personal data. We never share your watched companies with anyone.
