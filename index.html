About the founder

Why I built Provenexa

Sumit Jaswal

Founder, Provenexa Research Pvt Ltd

I spent 25 years in sales and business growth, across industries. Almost a decade of it was close to staffing and hiring.

In all those years, one problem never went away. Sales teams heard about a new plant, office or big order too late. They heard on the same day as everyone else. By then the first calls had been made. The best accounts went to whoever got there first.

The signs were always there, months earlier: a company registered, land bought, an approval, quiet hiring. But they were scattered across records and newspapers in many languages. Nobody had time to join them up.

Good salespeople are not short of phone numbers. They are short of a reason to call, at the right time.

What Provenexa does about it

We follow those first quiet steps, every day, across India. We check each one against its source. Then we tell your sales team which accounts are moving, why now, and who to approach. So you call first.
