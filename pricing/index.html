Questions

What is a "move"?

A sign that a company is about to grow in a place: a new plant, a new office, a new global centre (a GCC, a team a foreign company sets up in India), a hiring push or a new leader. Each move is dated and tells you where it came from.

What does "a week late" mean on the Free plan?

Free members see a move seven days after paid members. Timing is the whole point of Next Moves: the earlier you call, the fewer competitors you meet.

What is a "full read"?

Every move has a one-line summary for everyone. The full read adds what it means, where the opening is for you, the time window, and what we don't know yet.

Do you show phone numbers or emails?

No. On Plus and Team we show the person likely to own the decision: name, role and their public LinkedIn profile. Please check before you call. The profile link works with the contact tool you already use.

Can I change my accounts?

Yes, within your monthly swaps. A newly added account stays at least 30 days. Your list is yours: we don't ask for it and we don't share it.

Can I try the paid features first?

Yes. Every new member can use everything for 5 days, once. Companies can ask for a two-week pilot of the Team plan. The 5 days start once online sign-up opens.

What are founding member prices?

The prices on this page are for everyone who joins by 31 March 2027. You keep them for 12 months from the day you start, even if prices go up later.

How do I pay, and is GST extra?

Prices are before GST. Monthly or yearly; yearly gives you 2 months free. You can stop any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Online payment opens soon. Until then, company pilots are set up by email.
