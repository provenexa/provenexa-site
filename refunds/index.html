Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: DRAFT NOTE: date of publishing

DRAFT NOTE: Lawyer: check these against consumer law and the payment partner's rules. (Sumit OK'd the rules on 10 Nov.)

The Free plan and the free trial

  • The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to cancel or refund.
  • The 5-day trial is free and needs no payment. It ends by itself.

Monthly plans

  • Cancel any time from your account, or by mail to research@provenexa.com with the subject "Cancel".
  • You keep your plan until the end of the month you paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • We do not refund the month you are in.

Yearly plans

  • Cancel within 14 days of paying and we refund the full amount.
  • After 14 days we do not refund. Your plan runs to the end of the paid year.

Changing plans

  • Moving up takes effect at once. You pay only the difference for the days left in your current period.
  • Moving down takes effect from your next billing date. If you watch more companies than the new plan allows, the newest ones are paused, not deleted, until you choose which to keep.

Failed payments

If an automatic payment fails, you keep your plan for 7 days while it is tried again. After that, your account moves to the Free plan. Nothing is lost: upgrade again any time.

The full account brief

  • A full brief costs Rs 299, paid once, for one company.
  • If the public record has nothing real on that company yet, we tell you, and we refund the Rs 299 in full.

Charged by mistake

If you were charged twice, or charged after cancelling, tell us. We refund the wrong charge in full within 7 working days of hearing from you.

How refunds reach you

  • Refunds go back to the way you paid, through our payment partner.
  • Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the money.

Stopping automatic payments

  • Cancelling your plan stops future charges.
  • If you paid by UPI AutoPay, you can also cancel the mandate in your UPI app.

Company plans and pilots

Large-firm agreements follow the terms written into that agreement.
