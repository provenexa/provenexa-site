Help

Short answers to the questions members ask most. Tap a question to open it.

Signing in

I did not get a code

Look in your spam or junk folder. The code comes from research@provenexa.com.

You can ask for a new code once a minute. Each code works for a few minutes only, so use the newest one.

It asks for a work email

Please sign up with the email you use at your company. Personal addresses like Gmail cannot be used.

Only have a personal address? Write to research@provenexa.com and we will help.

The page is blank or stuck

Some browsers block part of the page. On Brave, turn off Shields for provenexa.com. An ad blocker can do the same: allow provenexa.com in it.

Then reload the page.

I was signed out

A sign-in ends after a while. Sign in again with a new code. Your accounts stay as you left them.

The page

What is a move?

A sign that a company is about to grow in a place. It can be a new plant, a new office, or a new GCC (a team a foreign company sets up in India). It can also be a hiring push or a new leader.

Every move has a date. The newest moves come first.

What do the stages mean?

GCC hiring

Set up, not hiring yet
The company has set up in India (a new India company or office), but we have not seen job ads yet.
Hiring quietly
Job ads for its India centre are out, but the company has not announced the centre yet.
Announced
The company, or a news report, has said the India centre is coming.
Opened
The centre is open and working.
Growing
An open centre is adding people or space.

Plant staffing

Applied for approval
The company has asked the government to approve a plant or an expansion. Not approved yet.
Planned
A plant or an expansion is planned, but building has not started.
Being built
The plant is being built or set up.
Started
The plant has started production.
Whose word is it?

Each move says where it came from:

The company's own word
From the company's own papers or its website.
Public record
From a public record, such as an approval.
Job ads seen
We saw the company's job ads.
Reported
From a news report, not the company itself.

For a news report, the date is the day it was reported. The event itself may have happened a little earlier.

Need the original source of a move? Tap "Ask us" at the bottom of the company's page. We send it to you by email.

What does the star (*) mean?

A star marks our estimate, such as when hiring is likely to start. It is not a fact from the company.

What do "Watching" and "Worth a look" mean?

Watching means the company has had fewer than two moves in the last 30 days. We keep watching it and add each new move.

Worth a look shows up to three companies moving in your area that are not on your list. We pick them by their latest moves, never by a score.

Your accounts

How do I follow a company?

Type its name under My accounts and tap +. Its moves then come first, under Your accounts.

Only you can see your list. We never ask for it.

I cannot find a company

Add it anyway and tap "Ask us to track it". We start watching it for you.

We see only the company name, never who asked.

Can I change my accounts?

Yes, within your swaps for the month. A company you add stays on your list for at least 30 days.

How do alerts work?

On every plan, we email you when a company you follow moves. You get one short email a day, and only on a day when one of them moves.

To turn alerts on or off, tick or untick "Email me when my accounts move" under My accounts.

Briefs

What is a quick brief?

Every company you follow has one, on every plan. You find it on the company's page:

Quick view
What happened, and when the latest move was.
What it means
The stage, your opening, and our view in two or three sentences.
We don't know yet
What is still missing, so you know what to check.
Ask on the call
One question to ask.

The dated moves, who can act and the likely person sit on the right.

What is a full account brief?

It is the long version for one company, written by us when you ask. It is for paid plans.

Tap "Get the full brief · Rs 299" on the company's page. It opens our payment page on Razorpay with the company name already filled in. We write the brief within one working day, from what the public record supports, and mail it to you. If the public record has nothing real on that company yet, we tell you and refund the Rs 299 in full.

Plans

Free or paid: what is the difference?

On Free, you see last week's moves for one category and one state. Your 3 followed accounts come the same day, with alerts and a quick brief.

Paid plans show every move the same day and follow more accounts. Compare the plans.
